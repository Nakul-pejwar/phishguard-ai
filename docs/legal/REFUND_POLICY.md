# PhishGuard AI — Cancellation and Refund Policy

**Last Updated:** October 2, 2026

At PhishGuard AI, we strive to deliver top-tier cybersecurity protection for your organization. This Cancellation and Refund Policy outlines the terms for managing subscriptions and requesting refunds.

---

## 1. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee
All new paid subscriptions (Pro and Team plans) are backed by our **14-Day Money-Back Guarantee**. If you are unsatisfied with PhishGuard AI for any reason within 14 days of your initial purchase, contact support@phishguard.ai for a full refund—no questions asked.

---

## 2. Subscription Cancellation
- You can cancel your subscription at any time directly through the **Billing Settings** in your account dashboard.
- Upon cancellation, your account will remain active with full paid features until the end of your current billing cycle.
- After the billing cycle concludes, your account will automatically revert to the Free Shield tier with no further charges.

---

## 3. Refund Processing
- Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method (Credit/Debit Card, UPI, Netbanking) via our payment partners (**Razorpay** or **Stripe**).
- Refund processing times typically take **5 to 7 business days** depending on your banking institution.

---

## 4. Enterprise Contracts
Custom annual enterprise contracts are subject to the specific refund and cancellation terms stipulated in the signed Master Services Agreement (MSA).

---

## 5. Contact Support
For any questions regarding billing, cancellations, or refunds, please email **billing@phishguard.ai** or **support@phishguard.ai**.
