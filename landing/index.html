Frequently Asked Questions

How does PhishGuard protect my privacy?

PhishGuard uses a Zero-PII logging policy. URLs are normalized and only the domain name alongside a SHA-256 hash are recorded. Query parameters, session IDs, and email bodies are never stored on our servers.

Does PhishGuard slow down my web browsing?

No. PhishGuard evaluates top verified global and Indian institutions locally using an in-memory allowlist with 0ms latency. Uncached domains are checked via high-speed asynchronous edge APIs under 50ms.

Is PhishGuard compliant with India's DPDP Act?

Yes. PhishGuard aligns with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 with automated data retention policies, right-to-erasure endpoints, and enterprise DPAs.
